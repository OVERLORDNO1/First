# Story Templates — the "build-up" engine

Every video is a small story with tension and release. These are the arcs the agent rotates.
Each is written to a strict beat sheet so the build-up always lands.

## The universal beat sheet (all templates use this)
| Beat | Time | Job |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | 0–3s | A line that stops the scroll. A question, a confession, or a pattern-break. |
| **Build-up** | 3–13s | Sink into the struggle. Make them feel *seen*. Raise the tension. |
| **Turning point** | 13–21s | The shift. The realization. The music lifts here. |
| **Payoff** | 21–29s | The reframe fully lands. Hope, but earned — not cheap. |
| **Signature + CTA** | 29–35s | "You're not behind. You're becoming." + one call to action. |

---

## Template A — "The 2am Thought"
Open on the exact anxious thought they've had at 2am. Build the spiral. Then the turn:
the thought isn't a fact, it's a tired brain. Payoff: what to do instead.

## Template B — "The Version of You"
A short story about a future version of them who made it — narrated in second person.
Build: how far away it feels. Turn: that version starts with the next small choice. Payoff: today counts.

## Template C — "The Thing No One Tells You"
Hook with a hidden truth ("motivation is a myth — discipline is just self-respect on a schedule").
Build the myth everyone believes. Turn: reveal the truth. Payoff: the freedom in it.

## Template D — "Rock Bottom → Rewrite" (Origin Story)
Anonymous, universal. A person at their lowest. Build the darkness honestly (no shame).
Turn: the smallest ember of choice. Payoff: they didn't become someone new — they came back to themselves.

## Template E — "Permission Slip"
Hook: "This is your permission to ___ (rest / start over / let them go)."
Build: the guilt that keeps them stuck. Turn: reframe the guilt. Payoff: freedom + a first step.

---

### Rules for every script
- Second person ("you"), present tense, short sentences. Written for the *ear*, not the eye.
- No jargon. If you use a psychological concept, translate it into a feeling.
- One idea per video. Cut anything that isn't the story.
- The turning point must be *felt*, not just stated.
