# Content Strategy

## Content pillars (rotate these)
1. **The Reframe** — take a painful thought and flip it psychologically ("I'm behind" → "I'm early in a long story").
2. **The Small Shift** — one tiny habit/mindset change with outsized effect (2-minute rule, urge surfing).
3. **The Hard Truth** — a compassionate but honest wake-up ("no one is coming to save you — and that's freedom").
4. **The Origin Story** — a short narrated story of someone who rebuilt (anonymous, universal, relatable).
5. **The Nervous System** — regulate before you motivate (breath, grounding, why willpower fails when dysregulated).

## Posting cadence (fits under $20/mo)
- **3–5 videos per week.** Quality over flood.
- Best slots (local time): weekdays **7–9am** and **7–9pm**; Sunday evening for reflective posts.
- Same video → all platforms, captions auto-tuned per platform.

## Growth loop
- **Hook is 80% of the game.** First 3 seconds must stop the scroll.
- End every video with the signature line → builds a recognizable brand.
- CTA rotates: "Follow for the daily rewire" / "Save this for the day you need it" / "Send this to someone who's stuck."
- Reply to every early comment as the page's voice (calm, warm) — the agent can draft these.

## KPIs to watch (monthly)
Watch-through rate, saves, shares, follower growth. Saves + shares > likes for this niche.
