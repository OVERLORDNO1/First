# Brand Identity

> Edit this file to make the page yours. Everything the agent creates flows from here.

## Page name & handle
**Mind Unstuck** — **@mindunstuck**
*(benefit-driven: the page that gets you unstuck. Use the same handle on every platform.)*

## One-line promise
> Small psychological shifts that quietly change everything.

## Who it's for
People in their late teens to 40s who feel stuck, overwhelmed, or "behind" — and want
grounded, real motivation backed by how the mind actually works, not empty hype.

## Voice & tone
- **Calm, not shouty.** Confidence over aggression. We whisper the truth, we don't scream slogans.
- **Human and honest.** We name the hard feeling before we offer the shift.
- **Psychologically grounded.** We lean on real concepts (habit loops, reframing, self-compassion,
  nervous-system regulation) — explained simply, never like a textbook.
- **Story-first.** Every post is a small journey with a build-up and a turn, not a list of tips.

Avoid: toxic positivity, "grind 24/7" hustle clichés, shame, fake urgency, medical claims.

## Signature line (spoken at the end of many videos)
> "You're not behind. You're becoming."

## Visual style
- **Mood:** cinematic, moody, hopeful. Golden-hour light, rain on glass, wide quiet landscapes,
  a single figure walking, city at dawn.
- **Color:** deep teal + warm amber + soft off-white text.
- **Typography:** bold clean sans (captions), occasional serif for the closing line.
- **Pace:** slow build, then a lift. Music swells at the turning point.

## The 30-second promise (content format)
Faceless, cinematic, story-driven vertical video (9:16), 25–45 seconds:
**Hook (3s) → Build-up (10s) → Turning point (8s) → Payoff/message (8s) → Signature line + CTA (5s)**
