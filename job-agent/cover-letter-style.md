# Cover Letter Style Guide

Use this to keep every generated cover letter sounding like you, not like a
template. Edit freely.

## Tone
- Direct, confident, no filler ("I am writing to express my interest...").
- Plain sentences over corporate buzzwords.
- 3-4 short paragraphs, under 300 words total.

## Structure
1. One line: the role, and one concrete reason it's a good match (not "I am
   excited about this opportunity").
2. One or two sentences per relevant past achievement, tied directly to a
   requirement in the job posting. Use numbers where the CV has them.
3. One line on why this specific company (only if there's a real, specific
   reason — skip generic flattery).
4. One line closing, no "I look forward to hearing from you" cliché.

## Hard rules
- Never invent experience, numbers, or skills not present in `profile.json`
  or `cv/cv.md`.
- Never copy boilerplate between letters — each one must reference something
  specific from the job posting.
- No exclamation marks. No "passionate about". No "team player".
- Match the reading level and vocabulary of the job posting itself.

## Signature block
Use contact.full_name and contact.email from profile.json.
